Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains how PopChunk LLC ("PopChunk," "we," "us") handles information when you use our iOS apps, including PopChunk, PushUpLock, PainPanda, Takeoff Check List and Super Basic Calculator (the "Apps"), and this website. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your personal information.

Information that stays on your device

Most of what you enter in our Apps is stored only on your device (or in your own iCloud account, if you use iCloud backup). This includes your goals, tasks, notes, checklists, calculations, workout history and symptom or health journal entries. We do not have access to this content.

  • Camera (PushUpLock): the camera is used to count repetitions in real time. Video is processed on your device and is not recorded, stored or sent to us.
  • Screen Time (PushUpLock): app-blocking uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple does not let us see which apps you select or how you use them.
  • Health information (PainPanda): symptoms and journal entries you record stay on your device. We do not receive, sell or share them.

Information we may collect

  • Purchase information. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat and Superwall to confirm purchases, unlock features and show subscription offers. These services receive an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history.
  • Usage analytics. Some Apps collect anonymous usage data, such as which screens are opened and whether onboarding was completed, to help us fix bugs and improve the Apps. This data is not linked to your name or email.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and performance data may be collected through Apple, if you have chosen to share them, or through our analytics tools.
  • Advertising. Some free versions of our Apps may show ads. Ad partners may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads, but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks you. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we keep your email address and message so we can reply.

How we use information

We use information only to provide and unlock App features, process and restore purchases, fix problems, understand which features are used, and respond to you. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your health or personal content for advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers who process limited data on our behalf under their own privacy policies: Apple (App Store, payments, iCloud), RevenueCat and Superwall (subscriptions), and our analytics and advertising providers. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Data retention and deletion

Content stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App. Anonymous analytics and purchase records are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. To ask us to delete any information linked to you, email dave@popchunk.com.

Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. To make a request, email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the App or on this page.
